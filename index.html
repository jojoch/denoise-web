Remove Background Noise: Video

Support

Remove Background Noise: Video cleans up the voice in your videos and audio recordings, right on your iPhone or iPad. On your Home Screen it appears as “Remove Noise”.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: lenityapp@gmail.com. Tell us your device and iOS version if something doesn't work.

Which files can I open?

Videos from Photos or Files, and audio files in MP3, M4A or WAV from Files.

Does the app upload my videos?

No. Everything is processed on your device, and the app works without an internet connection.

Why does it ask to access Photos?

Only when you save a result. The app asks to add the new video to your library and never reads the rest of it.

Does it cost anything?

No. No ads, no subscriptions, no account.
