Denoise

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 for the Denoise app for iPhone and iPad. Denoise is made by Veronika Zelinkova, contact lenityapp@gmail.com.

Summary

Denoise does not collect any personal data and does not send anything anywhere. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your videos and audio

Videos and audio files you open in Denoise are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, and we have no access to them. Temporary files created while processing are deleted when the job ends.

Photos and Files

You choose which videos to open with the system photo picker or the Files app, so Denoise sees only what you select. If you save a result to your photo library, Denoise asks for permission to add to it. It never reads your library.

Contacting us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and your message and use them only to reply.

Third parties

Denoise contains no third-party libraries or services that process data. Apple distributes the app through the App Store under its own privacy policy.

Children

Denoise collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
