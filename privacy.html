Remove Background Noise: Video

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 for the Remove Background Noise: Video app (“Remove Noise” on the Home Screen) for iPhone and iPad. It is made by Veronika Zelinkova, contact lenityapp@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not collect any personal data and does not send anything anywhere. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your videos and audio

Videos and audio files you open in the app are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, and we have no access to them. A working copy and temporary files stay in the app’s own storage until you start a new clip or delete the app.

Photos and Files

You choose which videos and audio files to open with the system photo picker or the Files app, so the app sees only what you select. If you save a result to your photo library, the app asks for permission to add to it. It never reads your library.

Settings on your device

The app remembers two small things on your device: whether you have tried the touch-and-hold tip, and how many results you have saved and when it last asked you to rate the app. They never leave your device and are deleted with the app.

Contacting us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and your message and use them only to reply.

Third parties

The app uses the open-source DeepFilterNet3 speech model and Apple’s Voice Isolation, both of which run on your device and send nothing anywhere. There are no third-party services, SDKs or trackers. Apple distributes the app through the App Store under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
